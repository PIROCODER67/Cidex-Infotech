Common questions

Is the price final?

The prices shown are starting points. Your final price depends on the scope of your project and is confirmed in a written quotation.

When does work start?

A project starts only after the quotation is accepted and any stated initial payment or onboarding step is done.

Are delivery dates guaranteed?

Delivery dates are subject to the contract for each project. They can move if approvals, materials or payments are late, or if changes are requested after approval.

What if I cancel?

Before work begins there is no cancellation fee, and payments received are returned, except agreed third-party costs that cannot be recovered where the law allows. In the first 7 days after work begins there is no cancellation fee. After that, charges depend on the contract, and the team explains any amount in writing.

How many revisions are included?

Each quotation states how many revisions are included. Extra revisions are charged only after you approve the extra charge.

What happens with late payments?

CIDEX generally sends one written reminder and makes one phone call within about 7 days of the due date. If it stays unpaid, work may be paused. No late fee applies unless it was agreed in advance.

Who owns the final work?

After full payment, you own the final deliverables made for you, unless the agreement says otherwise. Third-party fonts, images, plugins and APIs stay under their own licences.

Does CIDEX guarantee results?

No. CIDEX does not guarantee sales, leads, rankings, followers or revenue.
