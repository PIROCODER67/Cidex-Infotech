HOW TO READ THIS FILE
- Each question is answered from the customer's point of view: what the person facing the problem is going through.
- Your answers are kept in your meaning. Wording is tidied so the page writer can use it.
- [CHANGED] = I reworded or removed something. Check it still says what you meant.
- [CHECK]   = needs real proof, a rule check, or consent before it goes on the site.
- [ADDED]   = my suggestion, not from you. Keep, change or delete.
- Q7 is new in every block: what we must NOT say. This protects you from ad and professional-rule problems.
- Do not invent numbers, results or testimonials. Prices come from the price list, not from this file.

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Doctors, dentists, lawyers and chartered accountants in India have advertising rules from their councils (NMC/Dental Council, Bar Council of India, ICAI). A professional should check those pages before they go live.
- Any AI that looks at a photo and judges a condition is medical. It can collect information for a doctor to review, but it must not diagnose or recommend medicine.

   Q1. Who is the customer who finds you online?
